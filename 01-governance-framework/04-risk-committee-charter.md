# Technology Risk Committee — Charter

> ⚠️ Constructed case study. See [`../00-programme/disclaimer.md`](../00-programme/disclaimer.md).

**Approved by:** Audit & Risk Committee · **Effective:** current fiscal year · **Review:** Annual

---

## 1. Purpose

The Technology Risk Committee (TRC) is the management-level forum accountable for aggregating,
challenging and directing the treatment of technology risk across Northstar Global, within the appetite
set by the Board.

The TRC is a **decision-making body, not a reporting forum.** Its outputs are decisions: approve
treatment, direct escalation, accept residual risk within delegated authority, or refuse. A committee that
only receives updates is a meeting, and the risk it manages is unmanaged.

## 2. Authority

The TRC is delegated authority by the Audit & Risk Committee to:

- Approve the technology risk framework, taxonomy and assessment methodology
- Set and vary tolerance thresholds within Board-approved appetite
- Approve risk treatment plans and prioritise treatment funding within agreed budget
- Accept residual risk in the **Moderate** band (8–14) on the recommendation of the risk owner
- Direct first-line functions to remediate, including recommending a change freeze
- Escalate to the Audit & Risk Committee and, where warranted, the Board

The TRC **may not**: accept risk in the High or Critical bands; vary Board-approved appetite; approve
expenditure beyond delegated financial authority; or direct Internal Audit's work programme.

## 3. Membership

| Member | Role on committee | Voting |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chief Risk Officer | **Chair** | Yes |
| Chief Information Officer | Member | Yes |
| Chief Information Security Officer | Member | Yes |
| Chief Compliance Officer | Member | Yes |
| Chief Operating Officer (or delegate) | Member | Yes |
| Chief Financial Officer (or delegate) | Member | Yes |
| Head of Technology Risk | Secretary — convenes, papers, minutes, actions | No |
| Head of Internal Audit | **Observer** | No |
| Data Protection Officer | Attendee as required | No |
| Business unit representatives | Attendee as required | No |

**The Chair is the CRO, not the CIO.** A committee chaired by the executive accountable for delivering
the technology is being asked to challenge itself. This is the most consequential line in the charter.

**Internal Audit attends as observer without vote.** Voting would compromise the independence required to
later assure the committee's own effectiveness.

### Quorum

Four voting members, of whom at least two must be CRO, CIO, CISO or CCO. Decisions are by consensus; where
consensus fails, the Chair decides and the dissent is minuted. **Minuted dissent is a feature** — it is the
audit trail showing challenge occurred, and its complete absence over time is itself a warning sign.

## 4. Frequency

| Meeting | Cadence | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standing TRC | Monthly | 90 minutes |
| Deep dive (single category, rotating) | Quarterly | 60 minutes |
| Extraordinary | On escalation trigger, within 5 working days | As required |

Any voting member, or the Head of Internal Audit, may call an extraordinary meeting.

## 5. Standing agenda

| # | Item | Owner | Time | Output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Prior actions and decisions | Secretary | 5 min | Closure or re-dating |
| 2 | Aggregate exposure — inherent vs residual, movement since last | Head of Technology Risk | 15 min | Noted |
| 3 | Risks above appetite — status, treatment, acceptances due to expire | Risk owners | 20 min | Decision per risk |
| 4 | Tolerance dashboard — ambers and reds | Head of Technology Risk | 10 min | Direction on reds |
| 5 | Overdue treatment actions | Risk owners | 10 min | Re-plan or escalate |
| 6 | Control effectiveness — testing results, failed controls | Head of Technology Risk | 10 min | Remediation direction |
| 7 | New and emerging risks | Any member | 10 min | Accept into register or reject |
| 8 | Deep dive (quarterly) | Rotating owner | 20 min | Findings and actions |
| 9 | Escalations to Audit & Risk Committee | Chair | 5 min | Agreed escalation list |

Items 3, 5 and 6 are the ones that make this a decision forum. If a meeting passes through all three
without a decision being made or a date being challenged, the Chair should treat that as a signal that
papers are being written to avoid difficulty.

## 6. Papers and reporting

- Papers circulated **five working days** in advance. Late papers are deferred to the next meeting unless
  the Chair rules the matter urgent — this is the only reliable defence against decisions being taken on
  material nobody has read.
- Every risk paper states: scenario, category, inherent score, existing controls and their tested
  effectiveness, residual score, appetite position, recommended response, owner, cost and date.
- **A recommendation without a named owner and a date is not a recommendation and will be returned.**

### Reporting lines

| To | Content | Frequency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Audit & Risk Committee | Aggregate exposure, above-appetite risks, acceptances, escalations, framework changes | Quarterly |
| Executive Committee | Treatment resourcing conflicts, cross-functional dependencies | Monthly |
| Board | Critical risk, material incident, appetite breach requiring Board acceptance | On event |

## 7. Committee effectiveness

The TRC assesses its own effectiveness annually against measurable criteria, and reports the result to the
Audit & Risk Committee:

| Criterion | Measure | Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Attendance | Voting member attendance rate | ≥85% |
| Preparedness | Papers circulated on time | ≥90% |
| Decisiveness | Agenda items closed with a decision vs. carried forward | ≥80% |
| Challenge | Meetings with at least one recommendation modified or rejected | ≥50% |
| Follow-through | Actions closed by original due date | ≥85% |
| Escalation discipline | Above-appetite risks escalated within threshold timeframes | 100% |

The challenge metric is the unusual one and the most diagnostic. A committee that approves every
recommendation put to it is either receiving unusually good papers or performing ratification rather than
governance. Fifty per cent is a deliberately uncomfortable target.

## 8. Interaction with other forums

| Forum | Relationship | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Audit & Risk Committee | TRC reports to ARC | ARC owns oversight; TRC owns management |
| Change Advisory Board | CAB escalates change risk above threshold to TRC | CAB decides individual changes; TRC decides change risk posture |
| AI Steering Group | Reports AI risk into TRC | Steering Group decides AI investment; TRC decides acceptable AI risk |
| Incident Management | Material incidents notified to TRC | IM runs response; TRC addresses the underlying risk |
| Architecture Review Board | Consulted on treatment design | ARB decides technical approach; TRC decides whether residual risk is acceptable |

The recurring boundary is the same in every row: the other forum decides *what to do*; the TRC decides
*whether the remaining risk is acceptable*. Where that boundary blurs — most often with the AI Steering
Group, which has both budget and enthusiasm — the Chair restates it.
