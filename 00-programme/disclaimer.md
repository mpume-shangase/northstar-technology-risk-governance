# Disclaimer

**Northstar Global is a constructed case study. It is not a client, an employer, or a real organisation.**

Every figure in this repository — headcount, licensing position, baseline metrics, incident findings, cost
estimates and loss models — was created to demonstrate assessment and delivery methodology against a
realistic mid-market Microsoft environment. No real organisation, client engagement, employee or dataset
is represented, and no confidential information from any employer or client appears anywhere in this work.

## Why the scenario is constructed

Identity risk work is confidential by nature. Publishing a real risk register would breach the trust of the
organisation that commissioned it, and a redacted one is unreadable. A constructed scenario allows the
methodology to be shown end to end — appetite statement, scoring, treatment selection, residual position,
executive reporting — without either problem.

## What is real

- The frameworks: NIST CSF 2.0, ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A, the CRISC risk lifecycle, PMBOK practice.
- The Microsoft Entra capabilities described, and the licensing constraints that limit them.
- The technical configuration in the linked lab repository, which was built and tested in a real tenant.
- The reasoning: every scoring decision, treatment choice and accepted risk reflects how I would argue the
  case with a real Audit & Risk Committee.

If you are assessing this work: the analysis is mine, the scenario is invented, and I will say exactly
that in an interview. Anyone presenting this as client delivery would be misrepresenting it.
